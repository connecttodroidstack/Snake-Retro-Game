Snake Retro

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026 (updated)

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how DroidStack ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the Snake Retro mobile game for Android (the "App"). If you have any questions, contact us at connecttodroidstack@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The App shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to deliver and measure those ads, as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following data locally on your phone so you can continue where you left off:

  • Level progress, stars and best scores
  • In-game coins and the skins you unlocked or selected
  • Settings such as music, sound effects, vibration, game speed and daily reminder on/off
  • Achievements, daily reward streak, daily challenge results, best times and Endless high scores

This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. In-game coins have no real-world money value.

Daily reminder notifications

If you allow notifications, the App may show one local reminder per day (for example, that your next level or daily reward is waiting). These reminders are created on your device from your saved progress. No information is sent to us or to a server to do this, and the App does not use Firebase or any push notification service. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings → Daily Reminder or in your Android notification settings.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the App sends your unlocked achievements and your best scores (Endless high score and total stars) to Google so they can appear in your Play Games profile and on leaderboards. Your Play Games profile name and avatar may be visible to other players on leaderboards, based on your Play Games settings. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Signing in is optional; the game works fully without it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App may use Google AdMob, a service from Google LLC, to show ads: optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (for example, to continue after a game over or to double the coins from a level), and native ads on some menu screens. Ads are never shown during gameplay. To show and measure ads, Google may automatically collect information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address (which may give an approximate location)
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version
  • Ad interaction data, such as whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped
  • Diagnostic and crash information related to the ads

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy. Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob and your privacy

5. Your choices about ads

  • Watching ads is optional. You can always restart a level or go home without watching an ad.
  • Consent (Europe). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent management platform) the first time you open it. No ads are requested until this step is complete. You can accept, refuse or choose which purposes and ad partners to allow.
  • US state privacy. If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, the App may show a notice that lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Ad Privacy. This option appears when a privacy law applies where you are.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • If you refuse consent or opt out, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads, or no ads at all.

7. Data security and retention

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. We do not run our own servers and do not keep copies of your data. Data held by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights in Europe (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the legal basis for personalized advertising is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy. Limited, non-personalized ads may rely on legitimate interests, as described in the consent message. You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not store personal information ourselves, most requests are best handled by changing your choice in the App, clearing the App's data, adjusting your device's ad settings, or contacting Google. You can also email us and we will help.

10. Your rights in the United States

Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA) and other US states with consumer privacy laws (such as Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon) may have the right to:

  • know what categories of personal information are collected and how they are used;
  • access or delete personal information;
  • correct inaccurate personal information;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising;
  • not be discriminated against for using these rights.

We do not sell personal information for money. Sharing device identifiers with Google AdMob to show personalized ads may count as "sharing" or "selling" under some state laws. To opt out, use Settings → Ad Privacy in the App, or turn off personalized ads in your Android settings. Categories collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (advertising ID, IP address) and internet/app activity (ad interactions), disclosed to Google for advertising. We do not collect sensitive personal information. To make another request, email us; we will respond within the time the law requires and may need to verify your request.
